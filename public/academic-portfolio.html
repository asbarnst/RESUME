Nature & Collaboration Academic Project Group Work (Role: Lead Architect)
Level of Study 4th Year, 7th Semester B.Tech AI & Data Science
Date Carried Out September 2025 – November 2025
Technical Demonstration Full Structural & Technical Viability (Role-Based Security, REST API, Database Schema)

Smart Urban Traffic Management & Dynamic Signal Control

Author & Systems Developer: A. Mohammed Asbar

Project Name & Location Traffic Management System — Coimbatore Urban Study, India
Nature & Collaboration Academic Project Group Project (Role: Lead Systems Developer)
Level of Study 3rd Year, 6th Semester B.Tech AI & Data Science
Date Carried Out Academic Year 2024 – 2025
Technical Demonstration Real-Time Data Processing, Vehicle Monitoring Algorithms & Traffic Signal Timing Optimization
1. Design Concept & Inspiration

Inspiration: Static timer traffic signals cause severe urban congestion and unnecessary fuel consumption because green light durations do not adapt dynamically to real-time traffic density.
Goals & Objectives: Develop an intelligent traffic management system that monitors vehicle density at intersections, processes real-time traffic flow data, and dynamically adjusts signal green-time durations.

2. Concept Sketches & Dynamic Timing Control Logic
Vehicle Density Detection & Signal Control Loop (Diagram by A. Mohammed Asbar)
Figure 5.1: Real-time traffic queue length calculation and dynamic green-signal timing loop. Authored by A. Mohammed Asbar.
3. Technical Resolution & Viability

Resolution: Built in Python with data processing and algorithmic timing modules. Evaluated on simulated multi-lane intersection datasets.
Viability: Simulation testing demonstrated a ~28% reduction in average intersection waiting times compared to fixed-timer signal cycles.
